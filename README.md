# 💫 About Me:
I am an Information Technology undergrad at NIT Jalandhar, graduating in the class of 2028. I am passionate about software development and work comfortably with technologies like C++, Flutter, Firebase, SQL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I enjoy building a variety of projects, ranging from cross-platform mobile apps to low-level system tools. Some of my recent work includes ShelfLife, an app that uses ML Kit to manage household expiry dates; Medi-Quick, a utility that turns vital information into scannable QR code wallpapers; and a custom Network Packet Sniffer built with C++ and Raw Sockets.<br><br>Beyond building projects, I regularly practice competitive programming on platforms like LeetCode and Codeforces, and I love competing in hackathons like HackMOL and HackTU. I am also deeply involved in my campus community as an organizer for the Bawre dramatic club and a member of the Cybernauts Society.<br><br>When I am not coding in the vi editor on my customized Linux Mint setup, I enjoy creating digital and traditional art featuring Indian cultural motifs. I also spend my free time assembling drones, traveling, and playing asymmetric co-op puzzle games.
